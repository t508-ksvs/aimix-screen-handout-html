基礎一｜詞類與句子成分

【本章任務】能辨認常見詞類，並在句中定位主詞、動詞、受詞、補語與修飾語。
【核心概念】詞類說明單字的功能；句子成分說明它在這一句中負責的工作。
【判斷流程】圈主要動詞 → 找誰做事（S）→ 找承受者或補充說明（O／C）→ 標出修飾語。
1. Noun 名詞 1. 名詞 (Noun):
• 可數 N (cn): 若說話者視某物為固定整體，就是可數。(room, glass, chicken, paper)
　可數的話，記得加上 前 冠詞 a/an/the 或 後 複數字尾 -(e)s。
　例外：有些名詞的複數不太一樣，child → children，woman → women，sheep → sheep。
• 不可數 N (un): (1) 抽象　(2) 無固定形狀
• 名詞在句子當中可當 主詞(S) 或者是 受詞(O)。(詳見〈英文五大句型〉)
She is a beautiful girl. ／ They are students.
除此之外，若想要表示名詞的數量，也要考慮這個名詞是可數還是不可數。
可數不可數兩者皆可
many
a number of / a couple of
few / a few → fewer		 much
a ~ amount of
little / a little → less		 a lot of, plenty of, some, all, no, any, more
There is little water in the glass.
A great number of children are playing in the park.
名詞片語 / 短語 = (限定詞) + (形容詞) + 名詞。
例：a new book, many good students, his beautiful wife, those sweet roses
例外 (後位修飾)：something special
名詞字尾：creation, difference, friendship, kindness, government, ability
2. Verb 動詞 2. 動詞 (Verb)
• 及物動詞 (Vt) + 受詞 (Object): I love you.
• 不及物動詞 (Vi) + (介詞 + 受詞): I live in Taipei.
▲▼ 動詞的特性：必要性、變化性、一致性。 △▽

動詞三大特性 · 主動一致性練習

必要性：動詞在英文中佔有很重要的一席之地，是絕對必要、絕對不可缺少的。一個文法正確的英文句子裡，一定要有動詞。
Keep your room clean, John. ／ Be good. ／ It is important to have good habits. ／ I might be able to help you.
變化性：英文的動詞會因為動作發生的時間不同而有所變化，共有三態變化。
一致性：英文的動詞必須與主詞相互搭配一致，視主詞的人稱、數量來決定動詞。

【必會】基礎辨識 小試身手
1
My girlfriend is as beautiful (beautiful) as Susan.

比較級：當兩者互相比較時，使用比較級。(詳見〈比較級與最高級〉)
林俊傑、周杰倫超級比一比
JJ is younger than Jay.　JJ is taller than Jay.
JJ is more handsome (handsome) than Jay.
JJ’s songs are better / worse (good/bad) than Jay’s.
比較級變化規則
• 通常只要在形容詞的字尾加上 -er 就能夠形成比較級了，例如：shorter, taller, smaller。
• 或者子音+y 去y加 -ier，例如：happy → happier, easy → easier。
• 單音節，短母音，有時候需要重複字尾，例如：bigger, thinner。
• 若是字太長，就前加上 more 即可，例如：more beautiful。
• 但就是有一些不合群的傢伙，硬是要跟人家不同：
good → better, bad → worse, many/much → more, little → less
4. Adv 副詞 4. 副詞 (Adv)
用途是用於修飾 ( 動詞 )、( 形容詞 )、( 副詞 )、( 全句 )。
<!!!> 副詞在英文家族當中算是小媳婦，很沒有地位的，在句子結構當中可有可無，可省。
(1) Mary dances beautifully.
(2) My students are very nice.
(3) I am a very very …very smart student.
(4) Gradually, your English will improve.
(5) I rode the new bike happily in the park yesterday.
◆ 副詞跟形容詞是兄弟，長得很像，所以常常可以在形容詞後加 -ly 就可以變成副詞了，例如：badly, sadly, carefully, slowly
◆ 或者子音+y變成副詞，例如：easy → easily, lucky → luckily, 還有哪些例子呢？happily, heavily。
◆ 但是也有需要整形的，長得完全不像，例如：good → well。
◆ 還有形容詞、副詞長一樣，例如：early, late, hard 天生就是副詞的命，所以字尾沒有 -ly。
除了一個字的副詞外，還有副詞子句(詳見〈副詞子句〉)、時間副詞、地方副詞、(介詞+受詞)。

7. Aux 助動詞 7. 助動詞 (Aux)
用來幫助 ( 動詞 )。 助動詞可分兩種：真 (語氣情態)、假 (文法)
(1) 真心真意，永不變：(無條件幫助動詞) + 原動 (Vr~~)
(語氣情態助動詞) 表示猜測、可能性 (possibility)：
☆ 可能性高 must 一定 > will (>would) > should > can (>could) > may > might 可能性低
→ John must be very careless.    → It might be true.
(語氣情態助動詞) 表示義務、意願、責任、能力、許可強制性 (obligation)：
→ We must be very careful.
☆ 必須、一定：must / have to
　將會 (表示「願意，決心，承諾」)：will / would
　應該：should / ought to
　能夠可能 (表示「有能力，有權利」)：can / could
　能可能 (表示「准許，許可」)：may / might
　最好：had better
助動詞練習
A
I / He / You / They will go to the party tomorrow.
B
Your English must be excellent(傑出的).
C
You had better not sit in front of TV too long. (sit)
D
(大家來找碴) Cans John playing basketball? → Can John play basketball?
E
(進階) You should study hard. ／ You should have studied hard. (意思差異) → 後者表示「過去本該努力卻沒努力」，表後悔或責怪。
F
(進階) I would rather stay (stay) at home than go (go) out.
[貼心提醒]
1. would, could 和 might 這些看似過去式拼法的語氣助動詞，卻也可以用在現在時間或未來時間。(用以表達可能性較低，或者比較禮貌的用法)
2. 語氣助動詞如果配合完成式 (have+pp.)，表示的是「相對過去的時間」。
(2) 假仁假義，隨人變：(有條件幫助動詞，動詞要做變化)
(文法助動詞)：
• beV + Ving ~ (進行式)：I am talking. ／ You were sleeping when I called. (參見〈英文時態〉)
• beV + p.p. (被動式)：The shoes were made in Taiwan. (參見〈被動語態〉)
• have / has / had + p.p. ~~ (完成式)：I have been to Japan. ／ John has gone to China.
動動腦：請問 do, does, did 這三個自己生的助動詞，是真的還是假的？ 有何功用？ → 文法助動詞；用於否定句、疑問句及強調語氣 (例如 I DO love you.)。

8. 限定詞 8. 限定詞
(1) 冠詞 (a, an, the); 所有格 (my, your)    (2) 指示詞 (this, that, these, those)    (3) 數量詞 (參見〈詞類與句子成分〉)
(1) 冠詞所有格之後，一定要加名詞，組成名詞片語：a book, an egg, the students, my teacher
(2) 數量詞可接名詞，或是本身就可以當代名詞：one, each, both, any, some, several, all
A. Do you have any money?　I have some (money).
B. Who do you love, Mary or Susan?　I love both.
(3) One shotgun is not good. 與 A shotgun is not good. 語意有何差別？ → One 強調數量「一把」；A 代表「任何一把獵槍」。
(4) John is a boy that wouldn’t cheat. ／ John is the boy that cheated on the English test. → a boy 泛指一類；the boy 特指特定那一位。
(5) Do you have time? ／ Do you have the time? 哪句是問現在此刻幾點呢? → the time 是問此刻幾點。
(6) Would you mind if I open the/a window? 語意有何差別？ → the window 指特定的那扇窗；a window 指任何一扇窗皆可。

1
_____ other waiters in the restaurant have worked here longer than Clark; only Lois and Lana started working here before him. [108 會考]
(A) All(B) Most(C) Few(D) Some
→ (C) Few：由「只有 Lois 和 Lana 在他之前」可知，幾乎沒有其他服務生比他資深。
2
His English was poor, and he knew very _____ English.
(A) few(B) many(C) little(D) much
→ (C) little：English 為不可數名詞，且語意為「幾乎不會」。
3
No one thought James would appear at Katie’s party. So when he ____, everyone was surprised. [104 會考]
(A) would(B) was(C) had(D) did
→ (D) did：代動詞，代替過去式的一般動詞 appeared。
4
I didn't come yesterday _____ my illness.
(A) because(B) because of(C) despite(D) in spite of
→ (B) because of：後面接名詞片語 my illness；because 後須接完整子句。
5
Many teenagers have difficulty (in) communicating (communicate) with their parents.
→ have difficulty (in) + Ving（介詞 in 後接動名詞）。
6
Today, whale watching _____ a popular activity. (112 統)
(A) became(B) become(C) has become(D) will become
→ (C) has become：現在完成式，表達過去演變到現在的成果。
7
_____ the years, hundreds and thousands of tourists have visited Orchid Island every flying fish season. (113 統)
(A) About(B) Below(C) Over(D) Without
→ (C) Over：Over the years 意為「歷年來、多年來」，搭配現在完成式。
8
_____ its tropical(赤道的) location, there are many glaciers and ice fields high up on the mountain. (98 統)
(A) Because of(B) Spite(C) Even though(D) Despite
→ (D) Despite：介詞「儘管」，後接名詞片語。

(A) us(B) me(C) myself(D) mine

3

(A) me(B) myself(C) her(D) herself
→ (B) myself：受詞與前面的受詞 me 為同一人，須用反身代名詞。
4
Taking pictures _____ a good way for me to remember the experience. (100 基)
(A) to be(B) is(C) being(D) are
→ (B) is：動名詞片語 Taking pictures 當主詞，視為單數。

(A) it(B) that(C) there(D) this

6
Health experts say that such diets _____ not successful. (106 統)
(A) is(B) are(C) be(D) have

基礎二｜英文五大句型 (句型 1–4)

【本章任務】能由主要動詞判斷五大句型，分辨受詞與補語。
【核心概念】英文句子的骨架由動詞決定；先看動詞需要幾個必要成分。
【判斷流程】找主要動詞 → 判斷及物／不及物／連綴 → 檢查受詞 → 判斷後方是 O 還是 C。
我打你 第一大：S + Vt + O (主詞 + 完全及物動詞 + 受詞)
I love you.
The early bird catches the worm.
She didn’t know what she should do.
(課本例句) ／ (自己造句)
我快跑 第二大：S + Vi (主詞 + 完全不及物動詞)
Time flies. ／ Look!
A little girl was crying (on the street).
He waited (for me).
It rained (suddenly) (on my way home).
We work (hard) (every day).
(課本例句) ／ (自己造句)
第三大：S + Vt + O + OC (主詞 + 不完全及物動詞 + 受詞 + 受詞補語)
(常考動詞：make, get, keep, find, think, consider, leave)
You make me angry (adj).
He had his hair cut (pp.).
Don’t keep me waiting (Ving).
They call Ms. Chang a walking dictionary (n).
We elected John our class leader (n).
Give me a hug! (課本例句) ／ (自己造句)
第四大：S + Vt + IO + DO (授與動詞：主詞 + 動詞 + 間接受詞人 + 直接受詞物)
(常考動詞：give, bring, send, buy, show, ask, tell)
I will buy you dinner.
You should write your mom a letter of thanks.
(課本例句) ／ (自己造句)
